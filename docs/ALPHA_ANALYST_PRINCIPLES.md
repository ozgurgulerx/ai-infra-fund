# Alpha Analyst Principles

## Purpose

Steer the system toward a high-alpha AI infrastructure financial analyst, not a generic dashboard, chatbot, sentiment scraper, or stock screener.

The system's job is to detect, explain, and audit short-to-medium-term AI infrastructure equity opportunities. It should turn catalyst evidence into ticker-level implications, deterministic signals, risk-aware rankings, and advisory-only recommendation artifacts.

## Core Analyst Loop

1. Detect catalyst.
2. Link catalyst to AI ecosystem theme.
3. Map affected companies and tickers.
4. Connect first-order and second-order beneficiaries.
5. Validate with evidence.
6. Convert to typed `MarketEvent`.
7. Compute deterministic signals.
8. Rank candidates.
9. Apply risk, crowding, and invalidation checks.
10. Produce advisory-only recommendation artifact.
11. Journal outcome and evaluate.

## Prefer

- catalyst-driven analysis
- second-order AI ecosystem mapping
- supply-chain and capex propagation
- point-in-time correctness
- evidence-backed claims
- thesis freshness and contradiction tracking
- risk-adjusted setup quality
- falsifiable invalidation rules

## Avoid

- generic sentiment scoring
- generic stock screener behavior
- unsupported buy/sell claims
- LLM-generated scores or weights
- UI-first development
- overbuilt agent frameworks
- broad market commentary without ticker-level implication

## Primary Output

The primary analyst output is a daily high-alpha AI infrastructure brief containing:

- top catalyst events
- affected tickers
- theme propagation
- evidence links
- signal score
- risk flags
- invalidation condition
- confidence
- watch, accumulate, trim, or avoid label

## Feature Gate

Every feature must improve at least one of:

- catalyst detection
- evidence quality
- ticker/theme mapping
- signal quality
- risk control
- evaluation quality
- analyst brief usefulness

If a proposed feature does not clearly improve one of these, do not build it until the task is reframed.
